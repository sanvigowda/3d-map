        About this map

        Every building, road, lake, park and street lamp is placed from open map data on one metric grid centred on Bengaluru (38 × 34 km). Routes, hazard reports and risk values are drawn exactly as the SafeNisha app supplies them. The map itself does not estimate risk.

        Where building heights come from

        Real

        • Building footprints and positions
        • Roads, footways, rail and metro alignments
        • Lakes, tanks, drains, parks and green space
        • Street lamps that volunteers have mapped in OpenStreetMap
        • Neighbourhood, landmark and station names

        Generated

        • A ±15% height variation between neighbouring buildings where only an area estimate exists
        • Shading, haze, glow and the moving pulses on a route

        Limits

        • Most heights are area averages on a ~90 m grid from 2018, so individual towers are often too low and new buildings may be missing.
        • Street lamps are only the ones mapped so far. A street without a lamp on this map is not known to be unlit.
        • Flyovers and underpasses are drawn flat on the ground.
        • This is a prototype. A lower estimated risk is an estimate, never a promise of safety.
